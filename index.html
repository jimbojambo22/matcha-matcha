Matcha! Matcha! Rulebook

Overview

Matcha! Matcha! is a fast, light, and strategic card‑matching game for 1–6 players. Predict whether the next card will Match or No Match, build points, and decide when to bank them before luck runs out! After two rounds, the player with the highest score wins.

The Deck

The deck contains several types of cards:

  • Single‑Suit Number Cards (1–8 in each suit):
    Leaf, Cup, Whisk, Teapot, Blossom
  • Two‑Suit Cards — count as BOTH suits when matching.
  • Two‑Number Cards — count as BOTH numbers when matching.
  • Special Cards — Last Sip & Free Space (coming soon).

Objective

Earn the most points by predicting Match or No Match, banking wisely, and managing your steeped cards. The player with the highest total score after two rounds wins.

Turn Structure

  1. A starting card is placed on the table.
  2. The player must make their first guess (Match or No Match). You cannot bank before making your first guess.
  3. A new card is drawn and resolved.
  4. After at least one guess, the player may:
    • Keep guessing
    • Steep the Pot
    • Bank Points
  5. The turn ends when the player banks, guesses incorrectly, or triggers a Matcha‑Matcha.

Matching Rules

When a card is drawn, compare it to all cards on the table.

  • Suit Match (any suit on the drawn card matches any suit on a table card)
  • Number Match (any number on the drawn card matches any number on a table card)
  • Matcha! = Suit or Number matches
  • Matcha‑Matcha!! = Suit and Number match — instant loss of pot & end of turn
  • No Match = neither matches

Guess Outcomes

  • Correct (Match) — card is added; player continues.
  • Correct (No Match) — card is added; player continues.
  • Wrong Guess — pot is lost; turn ends.
  • Matcha‑Matcha — pot lost + turn ends immediately.

Banking Points

You may bank only after making your first guess.

Banking gives:

  • 1 point for every card on the table
  • +1 point for each hidden steeped card

Banking ends the turn and secures your points. In Score Token modes, banking also awards a token equal to the points banked (if available).

Steeping the Pot

Steeping lets you save up potential points for later. When you steep:

  • All table cards except the top card go into your hidden pot.
  • The top card becomes your starting “steeped card” for your next turn.

Steeping is a great way to build larger future banks—but risky if the pot is lost later.

Scoring Modes

Free Scoring

Bank any total (after your first guess). No restrictions.

Score Tokens

Banking requires collecting a unique token for the banked total (1–8). Once a token is taken, it cannot be taken again.

Trade‑In Tokens

Same as Score Tokens, but whenever a player ends their turn with 2+ tokens, they automatically trade them for a single combined‑value token if available.

Rounds and Direction

Matcha! Matcha! is played over two rounds:

Round 1 — Clockwise

Ends when fewer than 5 cards remain at the start of a turn.

Round 2 — Counterclockwise

Deck is reshuffled; direction reverses. Ends under the same conditions.

Winning the Game

After two rounds, the player with the highest total score wins. Ties can be settled by rematch, high‑card draw, or snacks—your choice!

Enjoy!

Steep boldly, match wisely, and may all your rounds be full of flavor.
